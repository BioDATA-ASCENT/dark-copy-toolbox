1The question

Copy number is usually measured by read depth: twice the reads, twice the copies. This breaks for genes with near-identical twins (paralogs), because a short read from one twin also matches the other, so the aligner gives it mapping quality 0 and most pipelines discard it. We asked: how well do read-depth and pangenome-aware methods count copies of such genes, on GRCh38 versus the complete T2T-CHM13 reference, when scored against copy numbers read directly from long-read assemblies?

SMN1 / SMN2 · chr5q13

SMN1 = 0 causes spinal muscular atrophy, and 1 copy makes a carrier. The genes are ~28 kb inside a 1–3 Mb inverted duplication whose structure differs between people. The twins differ at ~16 positions.

HBA1 / HBA2 · chr16p13.3

α-thalassemia. Common deletions (−α3.7, −α4.2) fuse the two genes into one. It's a small, GC-rich region.

CBS · U2AF1 · CRYAA · chr21

A reference error: GRCh38 contains a false second copy of these genes, so real reads are split between the two copies. CHM13 does not have the false copy.

2Ground rules we kept

  • No read mapping, ever. We used the public alignments as they are and streamed only the regions we needed (samtools view -L regions.bed -X cram crai). This is also why Locityper was not run (it aligns reads internally).
  • Never download whole CRAMs or whole assemblies. The only whole-genome files are the two reference FASTAs. Assembly regions were read remotely by byte range (samtools faidx on a bgzipped FASTA with local .fai/.gzi).
  • Downloaded files are data. We never ran code shipped inside downloads; for example, we wrote our own ctyper matrix subsetter instead of running theirs.
  • Idempotent scripts (*.ok markers), all settings in config/config.yaml, every judgement call logged in DECISIONS.md.

S10patients

Three simulated patients, each made of two real HPRC SMN haplotypes: SMA-affected (SMN1 0, SMN2 4), carrier (1, 3) and unaffected (2, 2). 150 bp paired reads at 10x, 0.3% errors, 400 bp fragments. Exact calls in 60/60 subsampled runs at 10x, 58/60 at 5x and 48/60 at 2x (the Bayes v0 model of 6b scored 60/59/53). The toolbox does the same counting in the browser on any FASTQ you drop in.

SMN caller

DRAGEN's built-in version of SMNCopyNumberCaller (Chen et al. 2020):

1 · pool the twins: reads on SMN1 and SMN2 are counted together at any MAPQ, so it no longer matters which twin the aligner picked;
2 · total from depth: pooled depth ÷ depth in normal two-copy regions × 2 = raw total (e.g. 4.01), turned into a whole number by a mixture model. Depth over exons 7–8 alone gives the full-length copies; the difference = SMN2Δ7–8 copies;
3 · split with the twin letters: at each SMN1/SMN2-distinguishing site, SMN1-letter read share × total = a per-site SMN1 estimate; the sites are combined into one SMN1 call and SMN2 = full-length − SMN1;
4 · flags: SMN1 0 → isSMA, 1 → isCarrier.

α-globin caller (4.2.7+)

One of the “targeted callers”. From depth patterns across the HBA locus it calls the common deletions and duplications (−α3.7, −α4.2, −−SEA, ααα3.7, ααα4.2 and others) and reports a two-allele genotype. We turn it into a total gene count (aa/aa = 4, -a3.7/aa = 3, aaa3.7/aa = 5, --/aa = 2).

Why three releases = one method

The SMN algorithm and the reads are the same. A new reference changes which twin a read is placed on, but step 1 pools the twins. The raw values agree to the second decimal (HG02668 total 4.94 / 4.93 / 4.93), and every call is identical. Accuracy: SMN1 205/206, SMN2 (incl. Δ7–8) 202/206, SMN total 201/206, α-globin 220/221, the same in every release. So the three scoreboard rows are not independent confirmations.

6bInternal experiment: the Bayes v0 caller (not in the deliverable)

StatusWritten overnight as a starting point for the team (caller/bayes_cn.py). It works, but it is not an independent method: its population prior and its SMN site-bias calibration both use DRAGEN 4.2.7 calls. So it was left out of the benchmark and the toolbox to keep the comparison clean. It is documented here so the team can build on it.

Idea

For a twin pair (A, B) = (SMN1, SMN2) or (HBA1, HBA2), try every combination of copy numbers cA, cB ∈ {0…5} and score how well each explains the person's reads, from two independent clues plus an optional prior:

log P(cA, cB | reads) =  log NegBin( depth | cA + cB )                 ← how many copies in total
                       + log BetaBinom( A-reads | A+B reads, p(cA,cB) ) ← how they split between the twins
                       + log prior(cA, cB)                              ← how common that genotype is (optional)
then normalise over the 36 cells → posterior; the call is the most likely cell

1 · Depth → total copies

Uses MAPQ ≥ 0 depth, because reads that can't be placed between the twins still count once in the sum: ĉ = CN(A) + CN(B). It is divided by a calibration factor, the median ĉ over all people ÷ 4 (it assumes the typical person has 4 copies per pair; no truth is used). ĉ becomes pseudo-read counts with the gene length, scored with a negative binomial whose extra variance comes from the person's own control-window noise (CV² scaled to gene length, plus a 3% floor).

2 · Twin sites → the split

At each twin-specific site (16 for SMN, ~20 for HBA) it counts reads with the A letter (x) and the B letter (y). Two corrections were essential:
• per-site bias ρ: some sites systematically over- or under-report one twin. ρ = median of (x/y) ÷ expected ratio over ≥10 people with x, y ≥ 3; expected = DRAGEN's SMN1/SMN2 for SMN, 1 (balanced) for HBA. Sites where ρ can't be estimated (e.g. never show B) are dropped.
• correlated sites: neighbouring sites are covered by the same reads, so the pooled counts x/ρ are shrunk to ~3 independent sites' worth.
Then a beta-binomial with expected A-fraction p = (cA(1−ε) + cBε)/(cA+cB), ε = 0.01 (read errors), concentration 150 (extra spread).

3 · Prior (optional)

Genotype frequencies of DRAGEN 4.2.7 calls among the other 649 people (leave-one-out, pseudo-count 0.5 per cell). For HBA DRAGEN only reports totals, so the prior is on the total with a uniform split. It helps a lot for α-globin (noisy depth) but pulls rare genotypes towards common ones. That's why the simulated SMA patients were genotyped without it.

How it was tuned (and what went wrong first)

  • The first version over-called α-globin (many people at 5 copies): two HBA "sites" were not twin-specific at all, and the ~20 sites were treated as independent evidence. Fixed by the site-bias step (which drops uninformative sites) and by shrinking to ~3 effective sites.
  • Defaults ε = 0.01, 3 effective sites and concentration 150 were set by hand on the overall data, not optimised on truth. The toolbox briefly had live sliders for them.

Ideas for a v1

  • Calibrate the site biases on assemblies (or leave-one-out on truth) instead of DRAGEN, which makes it fully independent.
  • GC correction of depth; model the SMN2Δ7-8 copies and SMN1/SMN2 conversions explicitly; per-haplotype output.
  • Fit ε, concentration and effective sites by cross-validation against the 206/221 truth people.

7Key results

  • The pipeline itself is fine: at the positive control RAD17, a unique single-copy gene in the same slice, plain depth is right for 223/223 people on both references, even with confident reads only. The failures below come from the twins.
  • Discarding ambiguous reads makes these genes invisible: SMN1 is right in 0% of people at MAPQ ≥ 20, on both references.
  • CHM13 fixes reference errors, not real twins: median CBS copy number at MAPQ ≥ 20 is 0.27 on GRCh38 and 2.27 on CHM13; SMN stays dark on both.
  • The twin-specific letters are what work: DRAGEN 99.5%, ctyper 91%. α-globin is harder for ctyper (76.5%), mostly by calling a third allele in GC-rich, noisy depth.
  • The truth is solid but not perfect: it agrees with DRAGEN in 201/206 (SMN) and 220/221 (α-globin) people. Of the 6 disagreeing people, 5 look like assembly errors (all read-based methods side with DRAGEN; see 6a), and one (NA20905) is a real DRAGEN miss of a partial SMN2Δ7–8 copy.

9Limitations

  • Healthy population: 1000 Genomes has no SMA patients (SMN1 = 0) and only 2 carriers among the truth people, so carrier detection is not really tested.
  • Not all comparisons are independent: ctyper's allele panel contains some HPRC people, so a few truth samples are not independent tests for it.
  • ctyper on slices: reads aligned outside our windows (e.g. ~5% of SMN reads sit on GRCh38 alt contig KI270897v1) are missing; it is not a whole-genome run.
  • Truth limits: it depends on assembly correctness (see HG00253) and is scored on totals for α-globin, because −α3.7 hybrids are one fused gene.
  • Small n: ~200 people gives 95% intervals of several points, so methods within ~4 points are not clearly different.
