Dark Dosage Explorer

How well does read depth count copies of genes that live in segmental duplications? Browse 1000 Genomes 30x read depth across SMN1/SMN2, the α-globin cluster and a GRCh38 false duplication on chr21, on GRCh38 and on T2T-CHM13, and check every caller against copy numbers read directly off HPRC release-2 assemblies.

Pangenome view

A local pangenome graph (pggb) built from complete HPRC R2 haplotypes plus GRCh38 and CHM13, anchor to anchor. Each row is one haplotype, coloured by our truth call. Hover a gene for details; click a row to open that sample above.

Caller lab

The Bayesian caller v0 runs in your browser: depth (negative binomial) + paralog-site allele counts (beta-binomial) + an optional population prior. Move a slider and every sample is re-called and re-scored against the assemblies.
